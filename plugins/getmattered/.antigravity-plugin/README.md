# GetMattered

GetMattered finds jobs that fit your resume, tailors your resume and cover letter for each role, and applies on the employer's own hiring system. It can send right away or wait for your approval.

## Set up

Install the plugin. On the first tool call your browser opens once, and you sign in with Google or an email link. There is no API key.

The plugin connects to `https://mcp.getmattered.com/mcp`.

## What you can ask

- Show me my best job matches.
- Apply to the best match for me and hold it for my review.
- What applications are waiting for my approval?

## What is in it

The connector reads your job matches, writes resumes and cover letters, sends applications and shows which ones the employer confirmed. The workflows are in `skills/`.

Your resume and profile stay on your GetMattered account. Tools read and write that account and nothing else.
